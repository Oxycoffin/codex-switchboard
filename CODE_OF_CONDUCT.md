# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We welcome respectful, constructive participation. Examples of positive behavior include being considerate, accepting feedback, focusing on the technical issue, and respecting different experiences and viewpoints.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, and misuse of project communication channels are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow these standards. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

To report conduct privately, use the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** flow and prefix the report title with `Conduct`. Do not include unrelated account credentials or personal Codex data.

This policy applies in all project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
